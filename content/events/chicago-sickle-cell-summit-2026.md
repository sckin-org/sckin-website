The Chicago Sickle Cell Summit brings together health system leaders,
providers, patients, caregivers, and community-based organizations to share
the latest advances in sickle cell science and to strengthen care across
Chicagoland. This year's theme puts clinical trials front and center: what
they are, how they are regulated and kept safe, why community support matters
for recruitment, and which trials are open right now to people living with
sickle cell disease in Illinois.

Keynote speaker Mark T. Gladwin, MD (Dean, Professor, and Vice President for
Medical Affairs, University of Maryland) is joined by featured speakers from
the University of Illinois Chicago and Northwestern University, including
SCKIN's medical director, Lewis Hsu, MD.

The summit is open to physicians, residents, nurses, pharmacists, social
workers, patients, and caregivers. The University of Illinois College of
Medicine at Chicago designates this live activity for a maximum of 3.0 AMA PRA
Category 1 Credits™.

The summit is hosted and organized by a volunteer Community Advisory Board,
patient advisors, and community-based organizations.
