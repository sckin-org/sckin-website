The 9th Annual Chicago Sickle Cell Summit is a free online symposium on
**Hope for Sickle Cell Through Clinical Trials**, hosted by the Community
Advisory Board at the University of Illinois Chicago (UIC) with a keynote from
Dr. Mark Gladwin. It runs from 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM Central Time (11:00 AM to
4:00 PM Eastern) as a Zoom webinar.

Clinical trials are how new sickle cell treatments move from the lab to the
clinic, and they depend on people living with the disease choosing to take
part. The summit looks at why trials matter and how patients and their
families can learn about them and get involved. It is open to everyone with a
stake in sickle cell care: patients, families, caregivers, advocates,
clinicians, and researchers.

Registration is free — sign up on Zoom below, and download the flyer to share
with your community.
