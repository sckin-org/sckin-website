criteria:
  heading: "How answers are rated"
  items:
    - term: "Medical accuracy (1–5)"

    - term: "Duty to inform (pass/fail)"
      definition: "does the answer give the person what they need to act safely — when to seek urgent care, when to consult their clinician, and the limits of the information?"
