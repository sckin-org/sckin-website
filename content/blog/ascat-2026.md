SCKIN is in London this week for ASCAT 2026, held at Central Hall Westminster
from 7 to 10 October.

On Thursday 8 October, Zacharie Liman-Tinguiri and Dr Lewis Thomas will
present **A specialized AI agent for sickle cell patient education in
low-resource settings: a benchmarking evaluation**.

- When: Thursday 8 October, 3:22pm to 3:34pm
- Where: Robert Perks Room, Central Hall Westminster
- Session: Oral Abstract Presentations: Healthcare Delivery & Innovation in
  Low and Middle-Income Countries (2:30pm to 4:30pm)

## What we studied

People living with sickle cell disease increasingly turn to AI tools with
health questions. We wanted to know whether a specialized agent answers
those questions more safely than general-purpose models. We put the same
sickle cell questions, in English and French, to SickleCellPedia and to
leading general-purpose AI models, and asked a clinician to rate the answers
without knowing which model wrote them.

The results we are sharing at ASCAT are preliminary. They come from a first
round of ratings, and we are now recruiting more clinician evaluators to
strengthen them.
