On July 23, 2026, SCKIN founder Zacharie Liman-Tinguiri was invited to speak at
Warrior Con 2026 — the 13th Annual Sickle Cell Warriors Convention, hosted by
the Sickle Cell Community Consortium at the Sheraton Gateway Los Angeles Hotel.

As part of the convention's "Sickle Cell Health & A.I." session, Zacharie
presented **Responsible AI Will Revolutionize Sickle Cell Care**: how AI built
responsibly — with medical rigor, transparency, and the community at the
center — can put trustworthy sickle cell knowledge in the hands of warriors,
caregivers, and clinicians everywhere.

Speaking directly with the warrior community about what responsible AI should
look like is exactly the conversation SCKIN exists to have, and we're grateful
to the Sickle Cell Community Consortium for the invitation.
