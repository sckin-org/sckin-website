On Thursday, September 24, 2026, the Community Advisory Board at the
University of Illinois Chicago (UIC) hosts the 9th Annual Chicago Sickle Cell
Summit — a free online symposium on **Hope for Sickle Cell Through Clinical
Trials**. It runs from 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM Central Time (11:00 AM to 4:00 PM
Eastern) as a Zoom webinar, with a keynote from Dr. Mark Gladwin.

Clinical trials are how new sickle cell treatments move from the lab to the
clinic, and they depend on people living with the disease choosing to take
part. The summit looks at why trials matter and how patients and their
families can learn about them and get involved. It is open to everyone with a
stake in sickle cell care: patients, families, caregivers, advocates,
clinicians, and researchers.

The event was shared with SCKIN by [Dr. Lewis Hsu](/about#board) of the
University of Illinois Chicago, our Medical Director and a member of the SCKIN
board. Putting trustworthy sickle cell knowledge within everyone's reach is at
the heart of what we do, and we're glad to help spread the word. Registration
is free — sign up on Zoom below, and download the flyer to share with your
community.
