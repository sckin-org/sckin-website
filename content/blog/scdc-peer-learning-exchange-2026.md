On Tuesday, April 21, 2026, SCKIN Executive Director Zacharie Liman-Tinguiri
and Dr. Lewis Thomas, SCKIN's Director for User Experience, presented at the
Sickle Cell Disease Coalition's Peer-Learning Exchange #5, a webinar held from
10:00 to 11:00 a.m. ET.

Their session, **Can AI Be Trusted for Sickle Cell Disease Education?
Introducing SickleCellPedia**, used the development of SickleCellPedia as a
case study for skills that transfer to any AI tool: how AI systems are
trained and tested, why they sometimes produce incorrect or misleading
information, and how to ask better questions to get more useful answers. They
also discussed AI as a decision-support tool in healthcare — its strengths,
its limits, and where it can appropriately support education and advocacy.

Helping the sickle cell community build the judgment to question and use AI
well — not just use it — is exactly the conversation SCKIN exists to have,
and we're grateful to the Sickle Cell Disease Coalition for the invitation.
